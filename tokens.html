How to Contribute

1. Choose a Token

Select one of the accepted tokens above. You can buy them on any major exchange (Binance, Bybit, etc.) and withdraw to your personal wallet (Phantom, Solflare).

2. Send to Treasury

Send the tokens to our official Treasury wallets:

  • Wallet 1: 4ovcwwTKYuCynQacrt8omamhjfkWo29A6iJBDYAbpSiB
  • Wallet 2: GRj75Ux6we8j9W2fa7kTiUtdNvFFXv7RqGDybJy2HoSs

3. Add a Memo (Important!)

When sending, you must include a memo with the Project ID (e.g., MEMO: flare-nigeria). If there is no memo, we cannot link your contribution to a specific project, and it will be returned or held in the general fund.

4. Track Your Transaction

Copy the transaction hash (TxID) from your wallet and paste it into Solscan.io to verify the transfer.

What Happens Next?

StepAction
1You send tokens with a Memo.
2Funds arrive at the Public Treasury (multisig).
3We verify the Memo and link the funds to the project.
4Funds are sent to the project wallet.
5Project submits a report (photo + GPS).
6We publish the report and the status becomes 🟢 Verified.

Important Rules

  • Not an investment: These tokens are receipts for contributions. They do not represent equity, profit-sharing, or financial returns.
  • No Memo = No Acceptance: Contributions without a valid Project ID in the memo will not be processed.
  • Multisig Security: Treasury funds are held in a multisig wallet. No single person can move them.
  • Right to Refuse: We do not accept funds from sanctioned entities or for projects without verification.
