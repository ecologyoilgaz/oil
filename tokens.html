How to Fund a Solution

1. Choose a Project

Go to the RWA & Liquidity page and pick a project that needs funding (e.g., Flare Capture in Nigeria).

2. Send Tokens to Treasury

Send the required tokens (USDT, €20, or $ONE) to our official Treasury wallets:

  • Wallet 1: 4ovcwwTKYuCynQacrt8omamhjfkWo29A6iJBDYAbpSiB
  • Wallet 2: GRj75Ux6we8j9W2fa7kTiUtdNvFFXv7RqGDybJy2HoSs

3. Add a Memo (Mandatory)

You must include a memo with the Project ID and your Solana wallet address to receive the RWA receipt token.

Example Memo: ID: flare-nigeria | MY_WALLET: YourSolanaAddressHere

4. Receive Your RWA Token

Once funds are received by the multisig, we mint your RWA receipt token. You can track the project's progress and the token's status (🟡 Funding → 🟢 Verified) in your Phantom or Solflare wallet.

Important Rules

  • Not an investment: RWA tokens are proof of donation/impact, not equity or profit-sharing.
  • No Memo = No Token: Without a project ID and your wallet address, we cannot issue your receipt.
  • Multisig Security: Funds are held in a 2-of-3 multisig wallet. No single person can move them.
