€ECO Impact Token (Universal RWA)

In addition to our specific project tokens, we offer €ECO — a universal RWA receipt. It allows donors to fund any solution in the ecosystem without needing to hold specific project tokens.

How It Works (The Flow)

  1. You Send Funds: You send $100 USDT to our Treasury and receive 10 €ECO tokens.
  2. You Tag a Project: In the transaction memo, you write the ID of the solution you want to fund (e.g., MEMO: flare-nigeria).
  3. We Execute: Funds are released to the project (e.g., installing flare capture).
  4. Satellite Verification: We check the satellite data (VIIRS). If the flare is gone, the status changes.
  5. Your Token Updates: The €ECO tokens in your wallet are now marked as "Verified Impact". They become a permanent ESG certificate.
