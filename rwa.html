Real World Assets (RWA) & Liquidity

Identifying a gap is not enough. We tokenize the solutions to attract liquidity. When you fund a solution, you receive an RWA token acting as a digital receipt and proof of impact.

How It Works (Step-by-Step)

  1. Select a Gap & Solution: E.g., Gas Flaring in Nigeria. Solution: Flare gas capture ($10M cost).
  2. Tokenization: We issue "Flare Capture Receipt" tokens on Solana. 1 Token = $100 of verified project funding.
  3. Funding: Donors/Investors buy these tokens via our Treasury.
  4. Execution: Funds go to the project. The project installs the capture unit.
  5. Verification: Satellite data (VIIRS) confirms the flare is reduced.
  6. Impact: The token status updates to 🟢 "Verified Impact" in your wallet.

Why RWA Tokens? (The Economics)

  • Transparency: Every dollar is tracked on-chain via Solscan.
  • Liquidity: Donors can see their impact token, which acts as a verifiable ESG certificate.
  • Accountability: Funds are released via Multisig only when milestones are met.
