Rules

  • No project ID — no acceptance. We return or ignore contributions without a destination.
  • Multisig only. No single key can move treasury funds.
  • Public audit. Every outgoing tx is published with a link to the project report.
  • No investment. Contributions are not securities, not tokens, not investments.
  • Right to refuse. We do not accept contributions from sanctioned entities or for projects without verified reports.
